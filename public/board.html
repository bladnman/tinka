How to use this board
Make yourself at home. Drag or scroll to move anywhere on the canvas, past the edges of the board too. Pinch, or hold Control or ⌘ while scrolling, to zoom; the zoom controls and Fit float at the lower right. With items selected, Fit (or F) frames the selection, then the whole board; ⌥-click fits the board. Back returns to where you were before a fit. The map button (M) shows the whole board: click or drag in it to move there. With the canvas focused, the arrow keys and Page Up / Page Down pan, Home and End go to the first and last exploration, and + / − zoom. Open an experiment to try it: it keeps this header pattern and the same zoom controls. Step through the others with ‹ › beside its title, ⌥← / ⌥→ (Ctrl+← / Ctrl+→ on Windows and Linux) or [ and ]; hover or click its title for where you are and what sits beside it. Escape returns here, as does ⌥↑ (Ctrl+↑ on Windows and Linux). Stars and notes are saved on this Mac.
