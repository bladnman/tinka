How to use this board
Make yourself at home. Drag or scroll to move anywhere on the canvas, past the edges of the board too. Pinch, or hold Control or ⌘ while scrolling, to zoom; the zoom controls and Fit float at the lower right. With the canvas focused, the arrow keys and Page Up / Page Down pan, Home and End go to the first and last exploration, and + / − zoom. Open an experiment to try it, then step through the others with Previous, Next or the dots. Escape returns here. Stars and notes are saved on this Mac.
